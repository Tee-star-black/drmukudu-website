Privacy

Cookie Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

Dr Mukudu & Partners uses limited browser storage and, when you consent, Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this website. We do not use the website analytics configuration for advertising or personalised advertising.

Essential storage

We use essential local browser storage to remember whether you accepted or rejected optional analytics. This allows the website to retain your privacy choice between visits.

Optional analytics

Google Analytics is loaded only after you choose “Accept analytics” in the cookie notice. Analytics helps us understand broad website usage, such as which pages are visited and how the site performs, so that we can improve the patient experience.

Your choices

You can reject optional analytics and continue using the website. If you previously made a choice, use the “Cookie settings” control on the website to reopen the consent notice and review your options.

Healthcare information

The public website cookie notice is separate from patient clinical systems. Sensitive clinical information should only be submitted through the approved patient and clinical platforms provided by Dr Mukudu & Partners and its authorised service providers.

Privacy and POPIA

For broader information about how personal information is handled, read the Privacy Policy. Dr Mukudu & Partners aims to process personal information in accordance with applicable South African data-protection requirements, including POPIA.

Contact

For questions about this policy or the practice’s handling of personal information, contact Dr Mukudu & Partners through the contact details published on this website.
